# ADR-0024 — A Role Task Notifies Every Holder

**Status:** Adopted · **Last updated:** 2026-09-01

**Decision date:** 2026-09-01 · **Deciders:** Product owner · **Supersedes:** — · **Superseded by:** — · **Related:** `D-48`, `D-39`, [ADR-0021](0021.%20AllowedBy%20Authorizes%20and%20Does%20Not%20Select.md)

## 1. Context

**Recorded retrospectively on 2026-09-01** from `D-48` in [Product Backlog](../../../projects/planning/02.%20Product%20Backlog.md) §6.1; the operative behaviour is `notification::service::role_recipients` (standard §9).

A notification has one recipient. `ROLE` and `DEPARTMENT_ROLE` assignments — the commonest shape in this product's fixtures and in [JWSS](../../schema/JSON%20Workflow%20Schema.md) §10's example — reach nobody in particular: the task is created unassigned with a `candidate_role_id`, and the inbox offers it to every holder until one claims it. #251 AC2 says *a task reaching somebody produces a notification for them*, so either every holder is told, or nobody is until a claim.

## 2. Decision

**One notification per current holder of the role, department-scoped where the task is.** The fan-out is unbounded, and the rows are not retracted when somebody claims.

## 3. Options Considered

| Option | Summary | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Notify every current holder** | Mirrors the inbox exactly | **Chosen** |
| B. Notify only the eventual claimant | One row, no waste | Rejected — not knowable when the notification must be written, which is inside the task-creation transaction (AC3) |
| C. Notify nobody until a claim | Avoids the fan-out | Rejected — a claim is somebody having *already* noticed; the notification arrives after it was needed, and the centre stays empty for the commonest case |
| D. Cap the fan-out | Bound the cost | Rejected — a cap silently chooses which holders matter |

### A. Fan-out — chosen

**The fan-out is the honest mirror of the inbox**, which is what makes it defensible rather than merely useful: everybody told is somebody the inbox will show the task to. `repository::holders_of_role` is written as the inverse of `workflow::repository::task::holds_role` — same validity window, same null-department reading ([ADR-0021](0021.%20AllowedBy%20Authorizes%20and%20Does%20Not%20Select.md)) — so the two cannot say different things without the drift being a bug in one of them.

**Rows go stale, and that is accepted:** once a holder claims, the others describe something no longer waiting for them. They stay — they were true when written, retracting them would delete rows from under somebody reading them, and *what was I told* would stop being answerable. **A notification is a record rather than a to-do**; My Tasks is the live view, one click away, and the centre says so on the screen.

## 4. Consequences

**Positive** — the notification centre works for the shape every fixture and the specification's own example use, and delegation needs no rule of its own: a window redirects the *task*, so `resolved.assignee_user_id` is already the delegate and the notification follows it (AC4 discharged by reading the task's own holder, which is what the `TaskArrival` struct exists to make impossible to get wrong).

**Negative, accepted** — a role held by two hundred people makes two hundred rows for one task. It is **unbounded**, and the place a person turns it off is FR-NTF-005 (preferences), which is unscheduled — named here so the limit is stated rather than discovered.

**Neutral** — a suspended account is not told: `holders_of_role` carries `status = 'ACTIVE'`, reusing `delegation_repository`'s predicate rather than reinventing it.

**Not settled** — whether a claim should mark the other holders' notifications read. It arguably should, and it is a change to rows this release writes rather than to the decision above, so it belongs with FR-NTF-005 or a later notification item.

## 5. Compliance

`a_role_task_tells_every_holder_of_the_role`. `holders_of_role` and `holds_role` are written as inverses of one another, so a divergence is a test failure rather than a silent difference between the queue and the notification.

## 6. Revisit When

FR-NTF-005 (preferences) lands, which is where a person bounds their own fan-out; or a deployment's role sizes make the row count a storage question rather than a design one.

## What Worked

**The sizing correction held on the kind of row it was written for.** [Construction plan 14](../planning/14.%20Sprint%2018%20Reporting%20Tail%20Construction%20Plan.md) §1.1 sized the widget rows against Sprint 17's widgets instead of older pull requests, because tests were half of every Sprint 17 widget. **FR-RPT-005 landed at 1.05–1.4× and FR-RPT-004 at 0.9–1.2×**, measured without generated files. [Retrospective 15](15.%20Sprint%2017%20Retrospective.md) warned that a rule should be measured on the work it next governs; this is that measurement, and the rule passed.

**A retrospective action became a row in the very next sprint, and it was in force before its subject.** Retrospective 15's first action, the browser-flow gate, landed as item 3b ([#456](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/pull/456)) at 06:15 UTC on 2026-09-14. **Both feature rows merged after it, and both shipped a browser spec**, taking `e2e/tests/` from thirteen to fifteen. Sprint 17 shipped three screens and no spec. The gate did not have to refuse anything; it only had to exist before the rows did.

**The close's first entry criterion ran first.** Construction plan 14 §7 step 1 required Sprint 17's close before construction, and it happened on 2026-09-13, before item 1. Plan 14 §6 was then amended against retrospective 15 before item 3 merged, meeting the deadline it set itself.

**A broken rollback was rehearsed, not reasoned about.** Item 2 ran the whole script six times on an isolated stack. Run 1 reproduced record 07's failure verbatim before the fix, and runs 3–5 went red on a mutated floor and on both wrong bundles. **The fix is known to work because it was seen failing first.**

**Both surviving mutations were recorded as findings and closed, for the fourth consecutive sprint.** Item 1's M4 exposed a scan branch no fixture reached, and item 5's J5 exposed a comment that promised failed-chart behaviour nothing tested.

## What Didn't

**The independent pass was not dispatched at mid-sprint refinement, again.** Construction plan 14 §7 step 7 put the dispatch there and predicted the outcome in writing: with the release moved to Sprint 19, nothing would force it. Nothing did. **The pass was dispatched on 2026-09-16, at the close, because the product owner chose it over closing `author-verified`.** **Sprints 17 and 18 are the first two sprints without a release since the pass was tied to the tag, and neither produced a pass at refinement.** Sprint 17 remains unread, and record 16's brief excludes it.

**The commit that documented the trailer gap has no trailer.** Item 6 ([#459](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/pull/459)) wrote into §2 that a reader's independence is checked by `git log` against `Claude-Session` trailers, and that nothing runs that check. **#459's own squash commit carries no `Claude-Session` trailer**, so no `git log` can say who wrote it. #450–#458 all carry one. The author is this document's author, which is known only because this document says so.

**Rows moved between sprints were recorded in the plan they left and not in the one they joined.** FR-RPT-006 was recorded as *labelled* in Sprint 19 when neither the issue nor the label existed; that was found and corrected on 2026-09-14. **#449 moved to Sprint 19 the same day**, is labelled `sprint:19`, and is still absent from the [sprint plan](../planning/01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md)'s Sprint 19 table, which says *there is deliberately nothing below this line*.

**An issue's premise went stale while it waited, and only the plan noticed.** #448 said the gate runs in a depth-1 clone. Item 3b changed that on 2026-09-14, and #448 was built on 2026-09-16 without its text being amended. **Construction plan 14 §8 carried the correction**, so the row was built on the right premise. It would not have been if the builder had read only the issue.

**A lockfile nearly misreported the sprint's largest row.** Item 5's pull request reads **4,007** added lines; 1,793 of them are `package-lock.json`. Against an estimate of 1,800–2,400, the raw figure says 1.7–2.2× and the real one says 0.9–1.2×. **The measure "added lines in the merged pull request" was never defined beyond those words**, and Sprint 17's table included 103 lines of `.sqlx` without saying so.

## Root Causes

**Every control that has worked binds a record or a gate that something reads. The two that failed this sprint bind a moment.** *Dispatch at mid-sprint refinement* and *add the session trailer* are both instructions addressed to whoever is working at that moment, and nothing reads either one when it is skipped. `sprint_reports_are_verified.rs` reads the report and made this sprint's exposure unsayable; it did not make the pass happen, as it said it would not. The trailer has no reader at all. **This is [retrospective 14](14.%20Sprint%2016%20Retrospective.md)'s finding a third time**, and the Sprint 19 release gate is the only thing that will bind the pass.

**A move is written by whoever is holding the plan being amended, and nobody is holding the other plan.** Plan 14 was the document open on 2026-09-14, so the move out was recorded carefully, with its reason. The sprint plan's Sprint 19 table is written by a different change, at a different time, and **no check reads a `sprint:N` label against the sprint's table**. FR-RPT-006 and #449 are the same defect in two directions: one row claimed where it did not exist, and one row existing where it is not claimed.

**A measure used in a table becomes a definition only when someone writes it down.** *Added lines* meant a PR's `additions` in Sprint 17 because that is what `gh` returns. It never had to be chosen until a row added a dependency.

## The sprint, in one paragraph

Sprint 18 built all seven of its rows and, for the first time in two sprints, built them close to what they were estimated at. The correction came from measuring Sprint 17's overruns instead of arguing about them. Its best result is quiet: a gate asking whether a sprint's screens are driven by a browser merged before either screen, and both screens then shipped a browser flow without the gate refusing anything. **Its worst is the same finding as the last three sprints**: the independent pass did not run at refinement because nothing forced it, and it was dispatched at the close only because the product owner chose that. **Its most specific is its own**: the row that documented that the criterion depends on commit trailers was committed without one. The pattern under the pass, the trailer and both one-sided sprint moves is one this project keeps meeting from different sides: **a rule addressed to whoever is working at the moment holds only when something reads the result.**

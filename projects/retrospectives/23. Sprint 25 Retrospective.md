## Root Causes

**Nothing tied a summed estimate to the capacity.** §3.1 rule 7 keeps each row's range and lets the sum absorb an overrun. The sum held: 30,284 inside 16,038–32,660. **But the sum's top was 11,946 over 20,714 when the plan was adopted**, and rows added outside the count had no capacity at all. A range that holds is not a sprint that fits.

**A row's campaign and its review gates are where its size goes, and a criteria-sized row has nothing to check that against.** Rows 48–50's tests were 79–89% of them. Each was sized from its own criteria with no merged comparable, as rule 7 forbids for the rows §3.2 sized.

**The merge control is still conduct, and conduct paraphrased it.** The convention is a first line that says *not ready*. When it became *Gates still to run*, it still told a person not to merge, and told no check anything. **Retrospective 21's root cause stands**: a control built in the repository and switched on outside it is not on until the switch is.

**A commit message is composed at the squash and checked only after the PR is opened.** The check exists in `scripts/check-commit-messages.sh`, and nothing runs it before CI does.

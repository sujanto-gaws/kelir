# Sprint 8 Status — 2026-08-27

**Status:** Final · **Last updated:** 2026-08-27

**Phase:** 4 Document Core (SDD §14) · **Sprint goal:** a published form definition becomes a form a person can fill in — rendered from the definition, evaluating its own rules as they type, and submitted through a server that does not believe what the browser computed.

## Summary

**The sprint goal is met, and the sprint's *exit* is met in two halves that nothing joins.** All six construction items are built and merged, the phase's centrepiece works end to end in a real browser, and the server recomputes every `calculate` and `conditional` expression before it persists anything — the security control this sprint existed to get right. What is not delivered is the sentence the exit uses to describe the loop: *"a user opens that type, fills the form … and submits it."* The renderer opens a **form** by form id (`/forms/:id`); the type-to-form binding is configured and read over the API and is traversed by no screen. Both halves are built, verified and demonstrable; the join is a route, and it is Sprint 9's — [#167](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/167) creates a document *from a type*. Recorded as a qualifier rather than as a pass.

**All four Sprint 7 findings still open against this sprint are closed inside it, which closes all seven** — [#200](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/200) and [#201](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/201) as construction, [#205](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/205) on 2026-08-26, and the four here — [#202](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/202), [#203](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/203), [#204](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/204) and [#206](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/206). Sprint 7 filed those seven and closed none of them.

**Status:** Final · **Last updated:** 2026-08-13

## Summary

**The sprint goal is at risk, and the gap is verification rather than construction.** Five pull requests merged and four more are green and awaiting merge; the code exists. But of the two Sprint 4 items previously recorded as delivered, **both failed Definition-of-Done verification**: the login rate limit was bypassable by a header the caller supplies and could be aimed at a third party's address, and the account lockout is permanent, so five unauthenticated requests can leave a freshly bootstrapped deployment with no administrator and no in-product recovery.

Neither defect was visible from CI. Seven rate-limiter unit tests passed throughout, because the limiter never saw a request — the same shape as Sprint 3's authorization sweep, which passes to this day while nothing binds a route to its permission string. Twelve issues were filed this sprint (#54–#60, #62, #65, #67–#69); five of them are decisions rather than work.

## Summary

**The sprint goal is met and every construction item is built. No item is `Done`.** All six items and the carried finding are merged on [PR #219](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/pull/219) with CI green, and the Phase 4 exit demo runs end to end in a real browser. But [sprint plan](../planning/01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §2 makes an item whose author is also its only verifier `In progress`, and **this sprint had no second reader at all** — every construction commit, every test and all three verification findings are one author's. Every row below therefore reads `author-verified`, which is what §2 and the [Sprint 8 retrospective](../retrospectives/06.%20Sprint%208%20Retrospective.md)'s third action require, and [record 06](../verifications/06.%20Sprint%209%20Surface%20Verification.md) §1 says so before it says anything else.

**Sprint 8's exit qualifier is closed.** That sprint's exit was met in two halves that no screen joined — the renderer opened a form *by form id* and the type-to-form binding was traversed by nothing. `NewDocumentPage.vue` is the join: a person picks a document **type** and the form they are given is whatever that type binds. Nobody types a form id anywhere, and `a-document-is-created-and-submitted.spec.ts` drives it.

**The predicate-coverage ratio rose for the first time since the campaign was invented to watch it fall: 48%**, against record 05's 22%, record 04's 37% and record 03's 65%. It clears [#217](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/217)'s bar. It did not rise by itself — the campaign ran twice, the first run's 30% was **discarded** because five of its eighteen reds were one flaky test rather than the predicates being mutated, and five of the surviving greens turned out to be [#218](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/218)'s category one sprint later and were closed with tests rather than filed.

**Three findings, one release-blocking, all three closed in the sprint.** Two of them refute claims this sprint's own commits had made:

- **F2, release-blocking**: a submit held **two pooled connections** on the gap-tolerant path, and twenty-four concurrent submits answered `500`. It is [#118](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/118)'s shape and this path carried it **twice** — once in the policy read, fixed when a test went red, and once in the allocation itself, twelve lines away, found only by re-reading against [coding standard](../../docs/standards/01.%20Coding%20Standard.md) §2.5. Recorded as **D-35**.
- **F1**: the concurrent-transition test was flaky, had been run six times green, and had been cited in a commit message as holding the compare-and-swap.
- **F3**: five predicates were the only guard on their behaviour, the worst being `replace_metadata`'s `document_id` — without it one metadata edit wipes every document's metadata in the tenant.

**Tests at branch HEAD: 913 passed, 0 failed** across 46 backend targets before the closing fixes, and the four document suites pass at 19 + 13 + 11 + 7 + 10 after them. Frontend: **410 passed** across 36 files. **Eight Playwright flows**, two of them this sprint's.

**The one entry criterion that was not met is the release rehearsal**, and it was not met in the way the [Sprint 8 retrospective](../retrospectives/06.%20Sprint%208%20Retrospective.md) asked: the rehearsal was to run *before* construction and it ran at the close. The criterion was written down, carried into this sprint's plan, and then not sequenced — see *Scope Changes*.

**Status:** Final · **Last updated:** 2026-08-12

## Summary

**Goal met.** All six Sprint 1 issues are closed and merged; CI is green on `main` at `f53860a`. The backend now boots, applies migrations, serves the four operational endpoints, and publishes a generated OpenAPI document. Verification ran against a real PostgreSQL rather than unit tests alone, which is what surfaced the sprint's three findings — none of them visible from reading the code.

The most consequential is that the eleven placeholder migrations were a latent trap and are now deleted: SQLx checksums every applied migration, so an empty `0002_identity.sql` recorded as applied today would have made SQLx **refuse to run** the moment Sprint 3 wrote its real DDL.

### Estimate against actual, feature apart from coverage

| Item | Estimated | Feature | Tests | Docs | **Actual** | Ratio | Tests as a share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <row: PR link> | <low–high> | <n> | <n> | <n> | **<n>** | **<actual ÷ high>–<actual ÷ low>×** | <tests ÷ actual> |

**The measure** ([retrospective 16](../retrospectives/16.%20Sprint%2018%20Retrospective.md)'s third action). [Status report 19](19.%20Sprint%2018%20Status.md) used it first, and this section writes it down unchanged:

- **Estimated** is the range the sprint's construction plan §3 gave the row. **A row nobody sized says `unsized`**, and it carries no ratio. An estimate is never written after the fact.
- **Actual** is the **added lines in the merged pull request, less generated files**. Generated files are `.sqlx/` query caches and lockfiles (`package-lock.json`, `Cargo.lock`). Deleted lines are not counted.
- **Actual splits three ways**, and the three parts sum to it:
  - **Tests**: `tests/*.rs`, `#[cfg(test)]` modules, `*.spec.ts` and everything under `e2e/`. A control that lives in a test file, such as a governance test, counts as tests.
  - **Docs**: Markdown files.
  - **Feature**: everything else.
- **Ratio** divides the actual by each end of the estimate. **Tests as a share** is tests divided by the actual.

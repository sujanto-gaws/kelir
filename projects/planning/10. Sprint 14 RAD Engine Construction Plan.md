# Sprint 14 RAD Engine Construction Plan

**Status:** Adopted · **Last updated:** 2026-09-04

This decomposes [Sprint Plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5's Sprint 14 into the order it runs in. It sits **one level below** that plan, whose entry and exit criteria it does not change; where the two disagree the sprint plan wins.

It is the sixth construction plan, after [03](03.%20Sprint%208%20Form%20Renderer%20Construction%20Plan.md), [04](04.%20Sprint%209%20Document%20Construction%20Plan.md), [05](05.%20Sprint%2010%20Workflow%20Construction%20Plan.md), [07](07.%20Sprint%2012%20Collaboration%20Construction%20Plan.md) and [08](08.%20Sprint%2013%20MVP%20Construction%20Plan.md). Related documents: the requirement authority is SRS §4.5 and §4.8, the schema authorities are [JFSS](../../docs/schema/JSON%20Form%20Schema.md) 2.0.1 with its [validation](../../docs/schema/JFSS%20Validation%20Rule%20Registry.md) and [calculation](../../docs/schema/JFSS%20Calculation%20Rule%20Registry.md) registries and [JWSS](../../docs/schema/JSON%20Workflow%20Schema.md) 1.0.0, and the evaluator decision is **D-10**.

## 1. Two things this plan says before anything else

### 1.1 The entry criterion is met for the first time in five sprints, and it is untested

**Every previous construction plan opened by recording that the independent pass had not happened.** [Plan 08](08.%20Sprint%2013%20MVP%20Construction%20Plan.md) §1.1 called its own planning the deadline for that, and the deadline passed. This one opens differently: [record 13](../verifications/13.%20Sprint%2013%20Independent%20Pass.md) exists, it covered all ten Sprint 13 items, and its reader authored none of them.

**That is not the same as the rule working.** Record 13 happened because the product owner directed a pass after the sprint had closed. §2 was amended on 2026-09-04 (`cdcd704`) to name the project manager as dispatcher, mid-sprint refinement as the point, and *a session appearing in no construction commit trailer for the sprint's range* as the criterion — and **it has never fired.**

**So the first thing this sprint tests is not a requirement.** Mid-sprint refinement is when the amendment either fires or does not, and the sprint plan's exit says a pass that runs on a direction rather than on the rule is recorded as the rule failing. **This plan cannot make it fire.** It can only refuse to schedule around it, which is why §7 puts refinement on the sequence as an event with a name rather than leaving it implied.

### 1.2 One item carries a published sentence, and that changes what cutting it means

**Item 4 — the document type and menu builder ([#341](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/341)) — is a `Should` with a released claim attached.** **D-64** accepted SRS §9 criterion 4 on the stated basis that this builder is scheduled for Phase 7, and `v0.6.0`'s release notes say so in text that has shipped.

Cutting a `Should` is ordinary. **Cutting this one makes a published sentence false**, and the difference is not rhetorical: the MVP was declared met partly on the strength of that schedule. Phase 7 has two more sprints, so the obligation is to Sprint 16 rather than to this one — but the row that is cheapest to drop is the row whose cost is furthest away, which is exactly how a promise like this gets missed.

**It is therefore above the line, and this plan says what happens if it slips:** it moves to Sprint 15 with the note attached, and if it slips again the backlog's criterion-4 row and D-64 are re-opened rather than left reading as satisfied.

## 7. Sequence

1. **Step 0** — §5's positions 5, 6 and 7 are open and named. They are not required to run first; they are required to be *placed* first.
2. **Item 1** ([#338](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/338)) starts immediately. It is the longest item and the only one anything else waits on.
3. **Items 2 and 3** ([#340](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/340), [#339](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/339)) may run in parallel with item 1 — different modules, no shared surface.
4. **Mid-sprint refinement** — **the independent pass is dispatched here, by the project manager, per §2.** This is an event on the sequence, not a note: if it passes without the pass being dispatched, the amendment failed on its first sprint and the retrospective records that.
5. **Item 4** ([#341](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/341)) starts once at least one of items 1–3 has merged, so the sprint is not carrying four open large items at once.
6. **The cut line is walked at the boundary**, with each row below it either built or dropped and the reason recorded — not re-decided at planning next time.
7. **Sprint close** — status report 15, retrospective 12. **No release**: Phase 7 exits at Sprint 16 with `v0.7.0`.

## 8. Decisions this plan takes, and the one it owes

**Taken here:**

- **The cut line is at seven**, against a sprint that just built ten, because this sprint's items are individually larger. §3.
- **Item 4 is above the line despite being a `Should`**, because its cost is a published sentence rather than a deferred feature. §1.2.
- **Conditional field visibility is below the line on a dependency argument**, not a priority one. §4.
- **[#339](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/339) covers the whole `taskType` vocabulary**, not only `SERVICE_TASK`. Four of six enum members currently behave as `APPROVAL_TASK` without saying so; closing one sixth of a silent-default defect leaves the defect.

**Owed, and not this plan's to take:**

> **D-66 — is the person merging inside the gate?** `main`'s protection is `enforcement_level: non_admins`, so adding required checks constrains everybody except the party who merges. The sprint plan makes answering it an **exit criterion** rather than a carried decision, which is the strongest thing a plan can do about a question it cannot answer. This plan notes only that [#335](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/335) cannot be closed without it: adding two contexts to a gate nobody is held to would satisfy the letter of the issue and change nothing.

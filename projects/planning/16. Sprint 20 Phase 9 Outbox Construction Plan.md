**Status:** Adopted · **Last updated:** 2026-09-18

This decomposes [Sprint Plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5's Sprint 20 into the order it runs in. It sits **one level below** that plan, whose entry and exit criteria it does not change; where the two disagree, the sprint plan wins.

It is the twelfth construction plan, after [15](15.%20Sprint%2019%20Phase%208%20Close%20Construction%20Plan.md), and **the second to open a phase**, after [13](13.%20Sprint%2017%20Reporting%20Construction%20Plan.md). Phase 9 is *Integration & Plugins* and ends at `v0.9.0` ([sprint plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §4). Related documents: the requirements are [SRS](../../docs/requirements/srs.md) §4.16 and §4.17; the outbox's shape is [Database Schema](../../docs/design/02.%20Database%20Schema.md) §12.8, the [Lifecycle Hook Contract](../../docs/schema/Lifecycle%20Hook%20Contract.md) and [ADR-0036](../../docs/architectures/adr/0036.%20The%20Hook%20Chain%20Ships%20Its%20Before%20Half%20First.md) §6; the sprint is shaped by **D-79** and **D-81**, and by two decisions this plan ~~raises and cannot take~~ raised, **D-90** and **D-91**. **Both were answered on 2026-09-18**, each with the recommendation as written, and so were **D-73** and **D-86**, which this plan placed (§8). **D-90's answer applied §8's swap** before any row had started: rows 5 and 6 moved to Sprint 21, and row 7 moved above the line (§4).

**D-97 asks whether Sprint 22 still carries FR-INT-003 beside the run** ([Product Backlog](02.%20Product%20Backlog.md) §6.2). ### 1.3 Sprint 21 is unread, and the tag waits for it

**Sprint 21 closed `author-verified`.** Every gate on every row was a subagent of the row's authoring session ([sprint plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §2 (b)). **That is 33,818 lines**, including the first code that resolves a secret, checks an egress address and calls out ([#592](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/pull/592)). **`v0.9.0` does not tag over it.** Record 20 reads it before the version bump, as **D-85** had record 17 read Sprints 17 and 19 before `v0.8.0` (§7 step 3).

**How §3.2 uses it:**

- **Feature** is the counted routes × 430, **plus any surface that is not a route**, sized against a named comparable (a worker, a client, a predicate).
- **Coverage** is sized at the share measured for the row's shape. That is **about 35%** for a module with pages and a browser flow (#543), and **50–67%** for backend-only work (#513, #514, [#451](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/pull/451)).

# Sprint 13 MVP Construction Plan

**Status:** Adopted · **Last updated:** 2026-08-31

This decomposes Sprint 13 one level down. It sits **one level below** the [Sprint Plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5, whose Sprint 13 section sets the scope and the exit; where the two disagree the sprint plan wins — **except where this document says the sprint plan is wrong, which it does twice and shows its working both times.**

**Sprint 13 is the MVP.** `v0.6.0`, SRS §9 in full, Phase 6 closed. That makes this the boundary where an unexamined surface and an undemonstrable claim cost most, and both are live going in.

---

## 1. Two things this plan says before anything else

### 1.1 The sprint's own entry criterion is unmet, and this planning was its deadline

Sprint 13's first entry criterion is *"Sprint 12 closed with its independent pass"*. **It did not.** [Status report 13](../status/13.%20Sprint%2012%20Status.md) is `Draft` for that reason, and it is the **fourth** consecutive sprint — Sprints 9, 10, 11 and 12. [Record 12](../verifications/12.%20Sprint%2012%20Surface%20Read.md) is an author's read and says so in its first sentence.

The [Sprint 12 retrospective](../retrospectives/10.%20Sprint%2012%20Retrospective.md)'s first action set **this planning** as the deadline, with an either/or rather than a fifth restatement:

> Sprint 13's plan names a reader who wrote none of it and a date, **or** §2 says what a self-pass is worth and `author-verified` becomes the expected label.

**This plan cannot name a party.** §8 records it as the decision owed, as [plan 07](07.%20Sprint%2012%20Collaboration%20Construction%20Plan.md) §15 did — and that is exactly what the retrospective refused to accept again. So the plan does the half it can: **it fixes the date** (§7, step 5), and it states the consequence of the date passing unmanned, which is that §2 changes rather than that a fifth sprint quietly repeats.

**A phase that tags its MVP on four sprints of author-verified work is a phase that tags on nothing**, and `v0.6.0` is the release this project has told itself since 2026-08-11 is the real one.

### 1.2 Two MVP criteria have no surface, and no sprint builds one

SRS §9 is fifteen criteria. Thirteen are built and demonstrable. **Two are not, and nothing in the plan changes that:**

| SRS §9 criterion | State |
|---|---|
| *Users can upload attachments* | The API exists ([#244](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/244), [#245](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/245)). **There is no screen.** `kelir-frontend/src/features/` has no `attachments` directory. Filed as [#295](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/295) |
| *Comments can be added* | The API exists ([#249](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/249)). **There is no screen.** Filed as [#296](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/296) |

Sprint 13's items 4 and 5 are the comment and attachment epics' **`Should` tails** — threading, edit, delete, categories, soft-delete, external references — and all of them are API work. Item 1 gives the *timeline* a screen. **Nothing gives attachments or comments one.**

**A criterion that says *users can* is not met by an endpoint.** This is the same defect as Sprint 12's exit sentence, one level up and with more riding on it: there, an exit asked for a browser demonstration of a feature with no browser; here, **the MVP's own acceptance criteria do**. Sprint 12's exit was rewritten. SRS §9 is not this project's to rewrite.

**§3.1 proposes the trade that fixes it**, and it is not expensive: two screens, paid for by the three items the sprint plan already nominates as the first cuts.

### 3.1 The trade this plan proposes

**Cut items 6, 7 and 8; add the two screens §1.2 needs.**

The sprint plan already nominates those three as the cut line — *"everything from item 6 down goes before anything above it is compromised"* — and all three are `Should`: master-data through workflow (**D-43**), the completed-task view (**D-43**), and the email channel. **None is an MVP criterion.** The two screens are two of them.

| Out | In |
|---|---|
| 6 — master-data changes through workflow (`Should`, D-43) | **[#295](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/295)** — attachments on a document: upload, list, download (SRS §9 criterion 6) |
| 7 — completed task view and task search (`Should`, D-43) | **[#296](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/296)** — comments on a document: read the conversation, add to it (SRS §9 criterion 11) |
| 8 — email channel and templates (`Should`) | — |

**Eight items becomes seven**, inside the measured rate for the first time in three sprints, and every remaining item is either a `Must` or an MVP criterion's surface.

**What the trade costs, stated rather than discovered:** three `Should` rows slip to Sprint 14, two of which **D-43** already moved once. That is a real cost and the alternative is worse — an MVP tagged on two criteria that no user can perform, discovered at the tag by whoever reads §9 rather than at planning by whoever reads this.

**This is a change-control deviation** ([Sprint Plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §8: scope enters through backlog refinement) and is recorded as **the decision §8 takes** rather than absorbed.

## 8. Decisions this plan takes, and the one it owes

**Taken:**

1. **Items 6, 7 and 8 are cut and two screens take their place** (§3.1), because an MVP criterion beats a `Should` and the sprint plan already named those three as the cut line.
2. **Two carried findings run before the items built on them** (§5), which is the first time this project has scheduled an allowance rather than pooled one.
3. **The MVP verification runs against release images** (§7 step 7), because the only two demos this project has run both found defects that only a deployment shows.
4. **The independent pass has a date** (step 5), placed after the MVP-critical items and before the tail, so a finding still has somewhere to be fixed.

**Owed, and it is the same one, for the third plan running:**

> **Who runs the independent pass.** The [Sprint 12 retrospective](../retrospectives/10.%20Sprint%2012%20Retrospective.md) set this planning as the deadline and said plainly that another restatement is not an answer. **If no party is named by step 5's date, §2 is what changes** — it says what a self-pass is worth, `author-verified` becomes the expected label rather than the exception, and this project stops claiming a control it does not exercise. That is a worse outcome than naming somebody, and it is a better one than a fifth sprint of pretending.

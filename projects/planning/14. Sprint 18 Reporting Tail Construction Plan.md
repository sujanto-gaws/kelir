# Sprint 18 Reporting Tail Construction Plan

**Status:** Adopted · **Last updated:** 2026-09-13

This decomposes [Sprint Plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5's Sprint 18 into the order it runs in. It sits **one level below** that plan, whose entry and exit criteria it does not change; where the two disagree the sprint plan wins.

It is the tenth construction plan, after [03](03.%20Sprint%208%20Form%20Renderer%20Construction%20Plan.md), [04](04.%20Sprint%209%20Document%20Construction%20Plan.md), [05](05.%20Sprint%2010%20Workflow%20Construction%20Plan.md), [07](07.%20Sprint%2012%20Collaboration%20Construction%20Plan.md), [08](08.%20Sprint%2013%20MVP%20Construction%20Plan.md), [10](10.%20Sprint%2014%20RAD%20Engine%20Construction%20Plan.md), [11](11.%20Sprint%2015%20RAD%20Authoring%20Construction%20Plan.md), [12](12.%20Sprint%2016%20Phase%207%20Close%20Construction%20Plan.md) and [13](13.%20Sprint%2017%20Reporting%20Construction%20Plan.md), and **the first since [10](10.%20Sprint%2014%20RAD%20Engine%20Construction%20Plan.md) for a sprint that neither opens nor closes a phase** — **D-81** moved `v0.8.0` to Sprint 19 on 2026-09-13, and the file was named *Phase 8 Close* until it did. Related documents: the release authority is [release process](../../docs/standards/04.%20Release%20Process.md) with its [checklist](../releases/00.%20Release%20Checklist%20Template.md), the requirements are [SRS](../../docs/requirements/srs.md) §4.14, and the sprint is shaped by **D-77**, **D-78**, **D-79** and **D-81**.

### 1.2 The table grew by three rows after the sprint plan wrote it, and the answer was a third sprint

**[Sprint plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5's Sprint 18 table was written 2026-09-12 and carries four rows above the line plus one below.** Since then: **[retrospective 14](../retrospectives/14.%20Sprint%2016%20Retrospective.md) landed on 2026-09-13 with five actions, two of which are Sprint 18 rows**, and **[#440](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/440) was filed the same day** — a reproduced defect in the rollback path.

**That is seven rows above a line the previous sprint overran at five**, and the sprint plan's exit criteria require four of them by name. **A plan that put seven rows above that line and called it a sprint would be doing what §3 of [construction plan 11](11.%20Sprint%2015%20RAD%20Authoring%20Construction%20Plan.md) did with one row and an inverted estimate**, at seven times the scale.

**It was not this plan's decision to make.** The item table is this plan's to order; **the exit criteria are the sprint plan's**, and three of the seven rows did not exist when those criteria were written. It was raised as **D-81** and **answered the same day: Phase 8 gets a third sprint.**

**What that buys is not room — it is separation.** `v0.8.0` moves to Sprint 19 and **the release leaves this sprint entirely**, so the two rows that must precede a release run are no longer in the same fortnight as the run they govern. **That tension is the one Sprint 16 item 1 was written to end** — *a control merged after the release it governs was never in force* — and [#412](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/412) applied it one step further out by sitting in the sprint *before* the sprint that tags. **D-81 is the third application and the first that did not have to argue for itself**, because the measurement in §1.1 was already on the table.

**The table this plan orders is therefore six above the line and one below, with no release in it** — FR-RPT-006 went to Sprint 19 with the tag rather than being cut, and D-79's six sites went below the line because they are the only row nothing depends on. §4 is that table and §8 records what D-81 settled.

**[Construction plan 13](13.%20Sprint%2017%20Reporting%20Construction%20Plan.md) §1.3 said exactly this sentence about Sprint 16 four days ago, and §6 of that plan now records what it cost**: retrospective 14 landed after all six rows had merged, so **not one of its five actions could be placed as a Sprint 17 row**. The plan's promise to amend itself was kept; the promise's *purpose* was not.

## 6. Where retrospective 15's actions land

**It is empty because retrospective 15 has not been written**, and Sprint 17 is open for that reason (§2, §1.3).

**[Construction plan 13](13.%20Sprint%2017%20Reporting%20Construction%20Plan.md) §6 made this commitment four days ago and kept half of it.** The amendment happened with its own diff; it happened **after every row had merged**, so no action became a row. **The commitment here is the same one with a deadline attached, which is the only part that was missing:**

**Retrospective 14's actions are not in this section**, because they are in §4 as items 3 and 7 and in §5 as status report 18's two rows. **That is the difference between this plan's §6 and the last one's.**

## 8. Decisions this plan takes, and the ones it owes

**Taken here:**

- **The widget rows are sized against Sprint 17's widgets, not against the pull requests Sprint 17 was sized by.** §1.1 and §3. The rule that a comparable is cited by number survives; **what is corrected is which comparable**, and the correction is measured rather than argued.
- **There is no designated in-sprint cut, because there is no release run to cut against.** The previous draft of this plan made item 5 the cut with a named trigger; **D-81 removed the trigger by moving the release**, and a cut whose trigger cannot fire is a sentence rather than a control. **The row below the line is item 7** and it is the only row nothing depends on.
- **Item 6 takes the amendment branch**: [sprint plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §2 is rewritten to state that a verification record's independence is **asserted by the record's own §1 and verified by nobody**, with the reason — commit trailers are not available to a depth-1 clone. **The instrument branch is not this sprint's row**, and **D-81 narrowed the argument rather than widening it**: the original reason was that a release run is the worst week to change how CI fetches the repository, and this sprint no longer has one. **What survives is the better half** — the instrument would assert that a *named reader* is absent from a commit range, and the gate that would carry it runs in Sprint 19. **Changing CI's fetch depth in the sprint immediately before a tag is the same objection one week later**, so the amendment is taken and the instrument is left to Phase 9's planning to weigh with full history in view. Retrospective 14's action permits either; it forbids a restatement, and an honest amendment is not one.
- **Item 7 ships without a test.** Five comment edits and an ADR sentence are a one-time correction, and a test asserting *no comment names the wrong phase* would need the phase number as a parameter and would go stale at Phase 9 — which is the defect it is meant to prevent, one document over. **[#402](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/pull/402)'s shape does not transfer**, and saying so is cheaper than discovering it.

Raised because the exit criteria were written before three of this sprint's rows existed and nobody re-counted them; answered 2026-09-13. **`v0.8.0` moves to Sprint 19**, FR-RPT-006 moves with it rather than being cut, and every later sprint shifts by one — Phase 9 to Sprints 20–21, hardening to 22–23, twenty-three sprints rather than twenty-two. **The two alternatives were named and rejected in the decision record**: re-baselining the exit so the phase could close with two widgets unbuilt, which contradicts the exit's own sentence that the dashboard SRS §4.14 describes *exists*; and moving the three new rows into Sprint 19, which is the sprint that tags and therefore the one place two release controls must not be. **This is D-16's, D-77's and now D-81's trade**, and the difference this time is that it was measured rather than estimated.

# Sprint 19 Phase 8 Close Construction Plan

**Status:** Draft · **Last updated:** 2026-09-16

This decomposes [Sprint Plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5's Sprint 19 into the order it runs in. It sits **one level below** that plan, whose entry and exit criteria it does not change; where the two disagree, the sprint plan wins.

It is the eleventh construction plan, after [14](14.%20Sprint%2018%20Reporting%20Tail%20Construction%20Plan.md), and **the first to close a phase since [12](12.%20Sprint%2016%20Phase%207%20Close%20Construction%20Plan.md)**. Sprint 19 tags `v0.8.0`. Related documents: the release authority is [release process](../../docs/standards/04.%20Release%20Process.md) with its [checklist](../releases/00.%20Release%20Checklist%20Template.md), the requirements are [SRS](../../docs/requirements/srs.md) §4.14, and the sprint is shaped by **D-81** and by three decisions this plan raises and cannot take: **D-83**, **D-84** and **D-85**.

> **`Draft` for two reasons, and it says both before anything else.** Sprint 18 is not closed, and record 16 has not landed (§1.1). **D-83** decides what item 4 is, and it is unanswered (§1.2). The plan goes to `Adopted` when §5 carries record 16's findings and D-83 has an answer.

## 1. Three things this plan says before anything else

### 1.1 Sprint 18 is not closed, and this plan breaks two actions to be written now

**[Status report 19](../status/19.%20Sprint%2018%20Status.md) and [retrospective 16](../retrospectives/16.%20Sprint%2018%20Retrospective.md) are `Draft`, waiting on [record 16](../verifications/).** The product owner asked for this plan on 2026-09-16, the day both were drafted.

**Two actions are affected, and neither is met by writing it now:**

- **[Retrospective 15](../retrospectives/15.%20Sprint%2017%20Retrospective.md)'s fourth action**: *a sprint's close lands before the next sprint's plan is written, or the plan records that it did not.* **This is the record**, which is the second branch. It is the fourth close in a row to arrive after the next plan. Unlike the last three, **the gap here is one record, not a retrospective**: every Sprint 18 fact this plan needs is already in the drafts.
- **[Retrospective 16](../retrospectives/16.%20Sprint%2018%20Retrospective.md)'s first action**: *record 16's findings that must precede the tag become `sprint:19` rows before construction plan 15 is written.* **They cannot, because they do not exist yet.** §5 has a slot for them instead, with a deadline (below).

**What it costs, stated so it can be checked at the boundary:**

1. **§5 is amended when record 16 lands, in a change of its own**, and every blocking finding gets a `sprint:19` issue in that change.
2. **The deadline is before item 4 starts.** Item 4 is the only row large enough to crowd out a finding. Items 1–3 are small, and nothing record 16 finds can make them wrong to build.
3. **If record 16 has not landed when item 4 is ready to start, item 4 waits.** The alternative has been tried: [plan 13](13.%20Sprint%2017%20Reporting%20Construction%20Plan.md) §6 promised an amendment without a date, and every row merged before it arrived.

### 1.2 FR-RPT-006 is not a widget until somebody says it is

**[ADR-0039](../../docs/architectures/adr/0039.%20A%20Dashboard%20Widget%20Is%20a%20Purpose-Built%20Endpoint.md) covers FR-RPT-001 through 005, and its decision says it covers the dashboard only.** The SRS calls FR-RPT-006 a *report*. It does not say where the report lives, whose approvals it counts, or what *approval time* measures. The [sprint plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) sized the row as though it were a widget, against [#437](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/pull/437), and nobody decided that.

**The answer changes the row's size by about two-thirds**, so it is raised as **D-83** rather than assumed:

| D-83 answer | What it builds | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **A — a dashboard card, the caller's own documents** (recommended) | A field on `DashboardSummary`: median and slowest time from start to final decision for the documents the caller raised. `reporting:dashboard:read` stays the only permission, and **D-82**'s invariant holds | **1,500–2,000** |
| **B — a report screen, tenant-wide** | A route, a page, a permission and its seed, and an ADR, because ADR-0039 does not reach it | **2,550–3,350** |
| **C — cut** | Nothing. Phase 8 closes on FR-RPT-001 to 005, and the row returns to the backlog, unscheduled | 0 |

**A is recommended because this is the sprint that tags.** B is the more useful report for a manager, and it is outside what [ADR-0039](../../docs/architectures/adr/0039.%20A%20Dashboard%20Widget%20Is%20a%20Purpose-Built%20Endpoint.md) §2 decided. It is also an ADR and a permission in the week of a release, which is the pairing [plan 12](12.%20Sprint%2016%20Phase%207%20Close%20Construction%20Plan.md) §1.1 exists to prevent. **If B is chosen, item 4 moves to Phase 9, not Sprint 19**, and the phase closes on a recorded decision, as the sprint plan's exit criterion allows.

**What *approval time* measures is part of D-83 for every answer except C.** The recommendation: **from `workflow_instances.started_at` to `completed_at`, for instances whose `outcome` is `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`.** A `RETURNED` outcome ends the instance, and a resubmission starts a new one (`uq_workflow_instances_live_document` allows one live instance per document), so a return is not an approval time. The row counts it as a finished instance with no decision, not as a long approval.

### 1.3 The release gate will pass on a record that has not read this sprint

**`releases_are_independently_verified.rs` requires `v0.8.0` to cite a record numbered above 15. Record 16 is numbered 16.** Once it lands, the gate is satisfied, and nothing in the gate asks whether anyone read Sprint 19.

**Record 16's brief reads Sprint 18 only**, and says so. So Phase 8 would reach its tag with **Sprint 17 read by nobody and Sprint 19 read by nobody**, on a gate that has passed. That is [#448](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/448)'s finding one step further out: the gate checks that a record exists, not what it read.

**This is D-85, and the recommendation is one more pass, over Sprints 17 and 19 together**, dispatched after item 4 merges and before the version bump. It would be record 17. It is the product owner's decision because it costs a session in the release week. **The alternative is to tag with two unread sprints and say so in record 08**, which is a legitimate outcome if it is written down.

## 8. Decisions this plan takes, and the ones it owes

**Taken here:**

- **Item 4 is resized to 1,500–2,000 on answer A**, from the sprint plan's 1,200–1,600, on #457's measured 69% coverage share (§3).
- **Item 4 is the designated cut, and its trigger is named**: record 16's blocking findings plus items 1–3 are still open when the release run must start. **Owed, and this plan cannot take them.** Each is raised in [Product Backlog](02.%20Product%20Backlog.md) §6.2:

- **D-83: what FR-RPT-006 is.** Where it lives, whose approvals it counts, and what it measures. **Needed before item 4 starts.** Recommendation: **A**, a dashboard card over the caller's own documents, start to final decision, `RETURNED` excluded (§1.2).

- **D-85: the release pass reads Sprints 17 and 19, or the tag records that they went unread.** **Needed before item 4 merges.** Recommendation: **the pass** (§1.3).
- **D-73, for a fifth sprint.** §5. Not a row and not forgotten.

### 1.2 FR-RPT-006 is not a widget until somebody says it is

**[ADR-0039](../../docs/architectures/adr/0039.%20A%20Dashboard%20Widget%20Is%20a%20Purpose-Built%20Endpoint.md) covers FR-RPT-001 through 005, and its decision says it covers the dashboard only.** The SRS calls FR-RPT-006 a *report*. **Answered 2026-09-16 by the product owner: A.** FR-RPT-006 is a card on the dashboard summary, over the documents **the caller raised**, under `reporting:dashboard:read` alone, so **D-82**'s invariant holds. **It shows the median and the slowest approval time, and how many documents they cover.** **An approval time is one per document**: from the `started_at` of that document's **first** workflow instance to the `completed_at` of the instance that ended `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`. So a document that was returned and resubmitted is timed across every round, from its first submission to its final decision. **The window is the last 90 days**, by the final decision's `completed_at`. Documents still in flight, and those whose last instance ended `CANCELLED`, are not counted. [ADR-0039](../../docs/architectures/adr/0039.%20A%20Dashboard%20Widget%20Is%20a%20Purpose-Built%20Endpoint.md)'s *This covers FR-RPT-001 through FR-RPT-005* is amended to include 006 in the row that builds it.

**As written before the answer:** **A is recommended because this is the sprint that tags.** B is the more useful report for a manager, and it is outside what [ADR-0039](../../docs/architectures/adr/0039.%20A%20Dashboard%20Widget%20Is%20a%20Purpose-Built%20Endpoint.md) §2 decided. It is also an ADR and a permission in the week of a release, which is the pairing [plan 12](12.%20Sprint%2016%20Phase%207%20Close%20Construction%20Plan.md) §1.1 exists to prevent. **If B is chosen, item 4 moves to Phase 9, not Sprint 19**, and the phase closes on a recorded decision, as the sprint plan's exit criterion allows.

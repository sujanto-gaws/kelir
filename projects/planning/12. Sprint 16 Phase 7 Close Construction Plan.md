# Sprint 16 Phase 7 Close Construction Plan

**Status:** Adopted · **Last updated:** 2026-09-09

This decomposes [Sprint Plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5's Sprint 16 into the order it runs in. It sits **one level below** that plan, whose entry and exit criteria it does not change; where the two disagree the sprint plan wins.

It is the eighth construction plan, after [03](03.%20Sprint%208%20Form%20Renderer%20Construction%20Plan.md), [04](04.%20Sprint%209%20Document%20Construction%20Plan.md), [05](05.%20Sprint%2010%20Workflow%20Construction%20Plan.md), [07](07.%20Sprint%2012%20Collaboration%20Construction%20Plan.md), [08](08.%20Sprint%2013%20MVP%20Construction%20Plan.md), [10](10.%20Sprint%2014%20RAD%20Engine%20Construction%20Plan.md) and [11](11.%20Sprint%2015%20RAD%20Authoring%20Construction%20Plan.md), and the third that also closes a phase, after [06](06.%20Phase%205%20Close%20Plan.md) and [09](09.%20Phase%206%20MVP%20Close%20Plan.md). Related documents: the release authority is [release process](../../docs/standards/04.%20Release%20Process.md) with its [checklist](../releases/00.%20Release%20Checklist%20Template.md), the decision this sprint discharges most of is **D-15**, and the sprint's shape is **D-76**.

## 1. Three things this plan says before anything else

### 1.1 The sprint has exactly one unconditional obligation, and it is not a row

**`v0.7.0` closes Phase 7, and the release run is work this table does not contain.** Every other sprint in this project decomposed into items whose sum was the sprint; this one decomposes into five items plus a release, and the release is the part that cannot be cut. [Release process](../../docs/standards/04.%20Release%20Process.md) §4 is a nine-step run with a staging rehearsal in it, and the last three releases each consumed a working session of their own.

**That is why the line is at five against a sprint that built seven.** §3 gives the measured sizes; the honest statement is that the unmeasured part of this sprint is the release, and a plan that budgeted six rows would be budgeting against work it had not counted.

### 1.2 Item 1 is the sprint, in the only sense that matters

**Phase 7's construction is finished and its process debt is not.** [SDD](../../docs/design/01.%20System%20Design%20Document.md) §14's five deliverables landed in Sprint 14, the builders `v0.6.0`'s notes promised landed in Sprint 15, and **D-76** declines to start Phase 8 here. What is left is the thing eight sprints have failed to do.

**The independent pass has never fired on its own rule.** [Retrospective 12](../retrospectives/12.%20Sprint%2014%20Retrospective.md) withdrew permission to restate it, Sprint 15 built `sprint_reports_are_verified.rs` against it, and [retrospective 13](../retrospectives/13.%20Sprint%2015%20Retrospective.md) recorded the eighth failure — on the sprint that built the test. **The test worked exactly as designed and did not help**, because it enforces a record and the missing thing is an act.

**So item 1 is not a process chore at the top of the list; it is the row the sprint exists to land.** [#392](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/392) permits two outcomes and forbids a third: the tag blocks on a verification record, or §2 is amended to say the pass is dispatched on request. **A reworded version of the present rule is not an acceptable close**, and the issue says so in its acceptance criteria rather than leaving it to a reviewer's judgement.

### 1.3 One row has no ceiling, and it is bounded by its own definition rather than by hope

**[#274](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/274) is an unexplained intermittent, and an unexplained intermittent is not an estimate.** It has been unscheduled for four sprints, which §5 of [construction plan 11](11.%20Sprint%2015%20RAD%20Authoring%20Construction%20Plan.md) already called *worth noticing*, and **D-75** then accepted in writing the cost of leaving it: since [#335](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/335) closed, an intermittent red in `Backend (Rust)` is a merge nobody can push through, including the party who merges.

**The row is a reproduction, not a fix, and that bound is the whole reason it can be scheduled at all.** A row that says *fix the intermittent* has no end state a plan can hold; a row that says *reproduce it, or record why it cannot be reproduced* has two, and both are closeable inside a sprint. **The precedent is [#361](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/361)**, whose mechanism could not be reproduced when finally tested — which is what bought [retrospective 12](../retrospectives/12.%20Sprint%2014%20Retrospective.md)'s fifth action and this sprint's release-checklist rule about follow-ups.

## 8. Decisions this plan takes, and the one it owes

**Taken here:**

- **The line is at five, and the sixth thing is the release.** §1.1 and §3. Every previous plan's items summed to the sprint; this one's do not, and budgeting six rows would budget against uncounted work.
- **Item 2 is bounded to a reproduction rather than a fix.** §1.3. A row that says *fix the intermittent* has no end state a plan can hold.
- **Item 1 runs before the release run, not beside it.** §7 step 3. If the outcome is the checklist trigger, a control merged after the release it governs was never in force — and this sprint has already seen that argument hold once, in Sprint 15's item 1.
- **The ADR is a criterion of [#393](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/393) rather than a third issue.** A record and the tier it accompanies are one review; splitting them would put a `Draft` ADR in the tree waiting for a merge that is already happening.
- **Item 5 is the designated cut.** Naming which row gets cut *before* the pressure arrives is the difference between a decision and a casualty, and §4 says which one it is.
- **This plan does not restate the independent-pass rule.** §7 step 6 names the event and says plainly that naming it again changes nothing. [Retrospective 12](../retrospectives/12.%20Sprint%2014%20Retrospective.md) withdrew permission and [retrospective 13](../retrospectives/13.%20Sprint%2015%20Retrospective.md) is the eighth failure; item 1 is the only instrument in this sprint that can reach it.

**Owed, and not this plan's to take:**

> **D-73 — whether Kelir grows an ERP layer.** The only row left in [Product Backlog](02.%20Product%20Backlog.md) §6.2, and [ADR-0037](../../docs/architectures/adr/0037.%20ERP%20Posting%20Runs%20Inside%20the%20Document%20Transaction.md) stays `Draft` behind it. **Nothing in this sprint waits on it**, and the part that could move without it — the three platform changes that are gaps in Kelir's own specification with or without an ERP, including the `POST` lifecycle stage [architectures/01](../../docs/architectures/01.%20Basic%20Framework%20Concept%20and%20Architecture.md) §12.1 specifies and nothing implements — belongs to Sprint 17's planning rather than to this plan. **It is named here so that the next plan inherits it explicitly rather than silently**, which is what [construction plan 11](11.%20Sprint%2015%20RAD%20Authoring%20Construction%20Plan.md) §5 did for [#274](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/274) one sprint before it was finally scheduled.

This decomposes [Sprint Plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5's Sprint 18 into the order it runs in. It sits **one level below** that plan, whose entry and exit criteria it does not change; where the two disagree the sprint plan wins.

Related documents: the release authority is [release process](../../docs/standards/04.%20Release%20Process.md) with its [checklist](../releases/00.%20Release%20Checklist%20Template.md), the requirements are [SRS](../../docs/requirements/srs.md) §4.14, and the sprint is shaped by **D-77**, **D-78** and **D-79**.

### 1.2 The table grew by three rows after the sprint plan wrote it, and this plan does not absorb that quietly

**[Sprint plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §5's Sprint 18 table was written 2026-09-12 and carries four rows above the line plus one below.** Since then: **[retrospective 14](../retrospectives/14.%20Sprint%2016%20Retrospective.md) landed on 2026-09-13 with five actions, two of which are Sprint 18 rows**, and **[#440](https://github.com/sujanto-gaws/kelir/issues/440) was filed the same day** — a reproduced defect in the rollback path.

**That is seven rows above a line the previous sprint overran at five**, and the sprint plan's exit criteria require four of them by name. **A plan that put seven rows above that line and called it a sprint would be doing what §3 of [construction plan 11](11.%20Sprint%2015%20RAD%20Authoring%20Construction%20Plan.md) did with one row and an inverted estimate**, at seven times the scale.

**It is not this plan's decision to make.** The item table is this plan's to order; **the exit criteria are the sprint plan's**, and three of the seven rows did not exist when those criteria were written. §8 records it as **D-80**, owed to the product owner, with a default that fires if nobody acts — the mechanism [retrospective 11](../retrospectives/11.%20Sprint%2013%20Retrospective.md)'s first action established and that has now worked twice.

### 1.3 Sprint 17 is not closed, and this plan is written anyway — for the second time, and this time the cost is known

**Status report 18 does not exist and retrospective 15 has not been written.** [Sprint plan](01.%20Sprint%20Plan.md) §2 requires them in the same change, so Sprint 17 is open by its own rule.

**[Construction plan 13](13.%20Sprint%2017%20Reporting%20Construction%20Plan.md) §1.3 said exactly this sentence about Sprint 16 four days ago, and §6 of that plan now records what it cost**: retrospective 14 landed after all six rows had merged, so **not one of its five actions could be placed as a Sprint 17 row**. The plan's promise to amend itself was kept; the promise's *purpose* was not.

**This plan does not repeat the shape, and the difference is stated so it can be checked.** Retrospective 14 **exists**, and its two Sprint 18 actions are **rows in §4 below** — placed before construction rather than after it, which is the thing that failed last time. **Only retrospective 15's actions are pending**, and §6 carries the same commitment with one addition: **the amendment is due before item 3 merges**, which is a date rather than a hope, because items 1 and 2 are the rows a retrospective about a release sprint is most likely to touch.

Naming it now is what makes it a decision instead of a casualty.

Retrospective 14's action permits either; it forbids a restatement, and an honest amendment is not one.
